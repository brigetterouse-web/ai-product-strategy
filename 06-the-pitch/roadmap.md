## Board Pitch

**Thesis:** a 1–3 person marketing team spending $200K–$1M a year can't fund the hire that would make that spend work, so we sell them that person's job, running.

**The case** — Why now: reasoning is good enough that the bottleneck is a confirmed plan; hosting moves the knowledge base to us; the hire is a $120–180k salary they can't fill. What's defensible: the plan each client confirms and owns, the campaign-ID trail from decision to revenue, their data behind an integration gate, and a benchmark that only exists because we host it. The engine itself is copyable. Economics: $2,500 against ~$455–730 COGS (~75–80%), rising to ~90% as oversight falls.

**The risks** — Trust: they publish under their brand, so provenance rules and an unwaivable gate. Scale/governance: no write-back, email drafted, liability stays with the client. Competitive: HubSpot Breeze. The honest one: no paying clients yet.

**The ask:** capacity to ship the server and land the first clients over ~3 months, hard review at month 3 against the H2 kill lines. *(set the figure and headcount)*

## M1 Baseline vs. Now

**M1 baseline:** "an AI marketing engine… own the professional-services marketing workflow end to end rather than sell another content tool."

**Now:** lean B2B teams will pay for a marketing function that runs and ties to a deal, because they can't fund the alternative hire. The durable assets are the confirmed plan, the attribution trail and a hosted benchmark; the engine is copyable. Priced against a salary, it holds ~75–80% margin. It's fundable because the risks are instrumented and we'd stop on named kill criteria; the open question is whether a client pays and the loop turns.
